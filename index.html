A branch built for Pampanga

Paeonia Medical Initiative is a student-led nonprofit working toward global health equity. Our branch brings that work home. We are students and young volunteers who listen to what our communities need, share health information that is accurate and easy to understand, and organize activities with local partners.

Kapampangan communities already know how to show up for one another. We want to give that bayanihan a modern form: organized, guided by evidence, and built to last.

What we do

Our priorities follow what barangay volunteers report, so the mix of activities can change from one area to the next.

Health education

Workshops and campaigns in schools and barangays on topics people in each area ask for, from sexual health and stigma to hygiene and mental health.

Barangay volunteer network

A volunteer in each barangay who listens, shares what the community needs, and helps run local activities.

Supply drives and fundraisers

Hygiene kits and essentials collected with local partners and handed over through trusted channels such as barangay health centers. We keep records of what comes in and where it goes.

Youth health briefs

Short, sourced reports on the health needs we hear about, shared every quarter so our work follows the evidence.

How our barangay network works

One volunteer per barangay is our long-term goal. We grow it step by step, one municipality at a time.

  1. Join

    A student or young adult signs up to represent their barangay or school.

  2. Listen and report

    Each month, volunteers send a short update on the health concerns and needs they hear about.

  3. Act on what we learn

    Every quarter we publish a brief and choose what to work on, checking facts with DOH data and barangay health workers.
